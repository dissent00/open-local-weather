Reading this page

Every error column is a mean stated as observed minus forecast, so a positive value means the model came in under what actually happened — a positive high error means it ran cooler than the day turned out, and a positive peak-wind error means it under-called the wind. Rain onset is scored only at Day+0, and only on days where rain was both forecast and observed; the extended outlook is never given onset timing, so a dash there is by design rather than a gap. Pressure trend is the 24-hour MSLP change, which carries a genuine physical lead on convection. Confidence reflects sample size alone: insufficient below 5 checks, provisional below 10, usable below 30, established at 30 and above. Not every model reaches every lead time — some models' forecast horizons end short of 7 days — so a blank row at Day+7 means no prediction was available to score, never a missed call.
