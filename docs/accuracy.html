Reading this page

Errors are stated as observed minus forecast, so a positive mean high error means the model ran cooler than the day turned out. Confidence reflects sample size alone: insufficient below 5 checks, provisional below 10, usable below 30, established at 30 and above. Not every model reaches every lead time — some models' forecast horizons end short of 7 days — so a blank row at Day+7 means no prediction was available to score, never a missed call.
